Local sky, ±30 days

Each dot is the position of the Sun or Earth at one hour in the window, as seen from the selected point. Left to right is the compass direction (azimuth), bottom to top is the angle above the horizon (elevation). Dots above the dashed line are visible. The large dots show the time on the clock.

When you zoom in, the Sun and Earth show as disks of their true size (Sun about 0.53°, Earth about 1.9°). The terrain hides the part of a disk below the horizon outline. "Around the Sun" and "Around Earth" use the same scale on both axes.
